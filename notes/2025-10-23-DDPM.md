- 生成是“涌现”or“幻觉”

思路：从一个简单分布采样是容易的

生成模型的解题思路

- 将观测数据分布映射到简单分布【Encoder】
- 从简单分布中映射到观测数据分布【Decoder】

#### why 高斯？高斯混合模型

一个复杂分布可以用多个高斯分布来表示

假设有K个高斯分布，这K个高斯分布称作混合模型的隐变量，则复杂分布的概率分布式：
$$
P_{\theta}(x)=\sum_{i=1}^{K} p\left(z_{i}\right)\ast p_{\theta}\left(x \mid z_{i}\right)
$$
这里$p\left(z_{i}\right)$表示第$i$个高斯分布在观测数据中占所占概率；

$p_{\theta}\left(x \mid z_{i}\right)$表示第$i$个高斯分布的概率分布函数



##### 实际K取多少？K是超参数吗？

得知$p\left(z_{i}\right)$表示第$i$个高斯分布在观测数据中占所占概率，那么$\int P(z)=1$

所以我们用一个**连续的标准高斯分布来表示**，$P(z) \sim N(0,1)$

上面离散的即转为连续（不需要告诉它取多少了）
$$
P_{\theta}(x)=\int P(\mathrm{z}) * P_{\theta}(x \mid z)
$$

#### 生成模型的优化目标（极大似然估计）

有一堆分布（dataset x），期望我们模型生成的这个东西和原始的这个x分布是尽量相似的

极大似然估计所求的这个$\theta$，就是满足$p_\theta(x)$  这个概率分布最大的时候，所取的这个$\theta$值

###### 如何求$\theta$？

**Maximum Likelihood Estimate**   $\quad \theta_{\theta}^{*}={ }_{\theta}^{\operatorname{argmax}} P_{\theta}(x)$

$q(z|x)$假设为一个概率分布，则
$$
\begin{aligned} {\log \left(P_{\theta}(x)\right)} & =\log \left(P_{\theta}(x)\right) * \int q(z \mid x) d z \\ & =\int \log \left(\frac{P_{\theta}(x, z)}{P_{\theta}(z \mid x)}\right) \mathrm{q}(\mathrm{z} \mid \mathrm{x}) \mathrm{dz} \quad \text { 联合概率与条件概率 } P_{\theta}(x, z)=P_{\theta}(x) * P_{\theta}(z \mid x) \\ & =\int \log \left(\frac{P_{\theta}(x, z)}{q(z \mid x)} * \frac{q(z \mid x)}{P_{\theta}(z \mid x)}\right) \mathrm{q}(\mathrm{z} \mid \mathrm{x}) \mathrm{dz} \\ & =\int \log \left(\frac{P_{\theta}(x, z)}{q(z \mid x)}\right) \mathrm{q}(\mathrm{z} \mid \mathrm{x}) \mathrm{dz}+\mathrm{KL}\left(\mathrm{q}(\mathrm{z} \mid \mathrm{x}) \| \mathrm{P}_{\theta}(\mathrm{z} \mid \mathrm{x})\right) \quad K L(p \| q)=\int p * \log \frac{p}{q} \\ & \geq \int \log \left(\frac{P_{\theta}(x, z)}{q(z \mid x)}\right) \mathrm{q}(\mathrm{z} \mid \mathrm{x}) \mathrm{dz}   \quad E(x)=\int xf(x)dx\\ & =\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{q}(\mathrm{z} \mid \mathrm{x})}\left[\log \left(\frac{P_{\theta}(x, z)}{q(z \mid x)}\right)\right] \quad \text { Lower bound 最大对数似然=最大lower bound }\end{aligned}
$$
(KL散度≥0)



## DDPM

Diffusion Model作为生成模型的一类，同样包含encoder和decoder两个阶段

- 前向扩散过程(Encoder)
  向观测数据中逐步加入噪声，直到观测数据变成高斯分布;      <img src="C:\Users\admin\AppData\Roaming\Typora\typora-user-images\image-20251023111636360.png" alt="image-20251023111636360" style="zoom:50%;" />

- 反向生成过程(Decoder)
  从一个高斯分布中采样，逐步消除噪声，直到变成清晰数据



1. 如何加噪声?

$$
x_{t}=\sqrt{1-\beta_{t}} * x_{t-1}+\sqrt{\beta_{t}} * \varepsilon_{t-1}
$$

2. 加了多少次噪声？

   在DDPM里一共加了1000次噪声

![image-20251023112008824](C:\Users\admin\AppData\Roaming\Typora\typora-user-images\image-20251023112008824.png)

- 重参数采样

若 y 是一个高斯分布  $y \sim N\left(\mu, \sigma^{2}\right) $， 则$\frac{\mathrm{y}-\mu}{\sigma} \sim N(0,1)$

设  $\varepsilon  $为一个标准高斯分布，则  $y=\sigma * \varepsilon+\mu \sim N\left(\mu, \sigma^{2}\right)$


所以  $x_{t}$  满足高斯分布，且$x_{t} \sim N\left(\sqrt{1-\beta_{t}} * x_{t-1}, \beta_{t}\right)$

<img src="C:\Users\admin\AppData\Roaming\Typora\typora-user-images\image-20251023112327786.png" alt="image-20251023112327786" style="zoom:50%;" />

- Forward Diffusion Process

  定义
  - 一个真实分布的data，分布满足  $\mathrm{x}_{0} \sim \mathrm{q}(\mathrm{x}) $
  - 共加了$T$次噪声，得到 $ x_{1}, x_{2}, \ldots, x_{T} $

  每次加噪声操作是对前一次加完噪声的结果操作（Markov chain），

  $q\left(x_{t} \mid x_{t-1}\right)=N\left(x_{t} ; \sqrt{1-\beta_{t}} * x_{t-1}, \beta_{t} I\right)$

  $ \beta_{t}$  是超参数,  在DDPM中， $ \beta_{t}$  是随着$t$线性增长的（随着$t$的增大， $x_{t}$ 趋近于标准高斯分布）

<img src="C:\Users\admin\AppData\Roaming\Typora\typora-user-images\image-20251023113156902.png" alt="image-20251023113156902" style="zoom:50%;" />

 $x_{t}$  可以根据  $x_{0}$, $t$ 直接推导出来
$$
\begin{array}{l}
x_{t}=\sqrt{1-\beta_{t}} * x_{t-1}+\sqrt{\beta_{t}} * \varepsilon_{t-1} \\
x_{t}=\sqrt{1-\beta_{t}} *\left(\sqrt{1-\beta_{t-1}} * x_{t-2}+\sqrt{\beta_{t-1}} * \varepsilon_{t-2}\right)+\sqrt{\beta_{t}} * \varepsilon_{t-1}
\end{array}
$$
展开
$$
x_{t}=\sqrt{\left(1-\beta_{t}\right) *\left(1-\beta_{t-1}\right)} * x_{t-2}+\sqrt{\left(1-\beta_{t}\right) \beta_{t-1}} * \varepsilon_{t-2}+\sqrt{\beta_{t}} * \varepsilon_{t-1}
$$
这里我们令  $\alpha_{t}=1-\beta_{t} $
$$
\begin{array}{l}
x_{t}=\sqrt{\left(\alpha_{t}\right) *\left(\alpha_{t-1}\right)} * x_{t-2}+\sqrt{\alpha_{t}\left(1-\alpha_{t-1}\right)} * \varepsilon_{t-2}+\sqrt{1-\alpha_{t}} * \varepsilon_{t-1} \\
x_{t}=\sqrt{\left(\alpha_{t}\right) *\left(\alpha_{t-1}\right)} * x_{t-2}+\sqrt{1-\alpha_{t} * \alpha_{t-1}} * \varepsilon \\
\cdots \\
x_{t}=\sqrt{\overline{\alpha_{t}}} * x_{0}+\sqrt{1-\overline{\alpha_{t}}} * \varepsilon
\end{array}
$$

其中  $\overline{\alpha_{t}}=\prod_{i=1}^{t} \alpha_{i} $

两个独立高斯分布的和依然是高斯分布，且均值为二者均值的和方差为二者方差的和。

给定图片和时间$t$，前向扩散是一个确定的过程
$$
\begin{array}{l}
q\left(x_{t} \mid x_{0}\right)=N\left(x_{t} ; \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t}} * x_{t-1}, \bar{\alpha}_{t}\right) = N\left(x_{t} ; \sqrt{\bar{\partial}_{t}} \cdot x_{0},\left(1-\bar{\partial}_{t}\right) I\right)\\
\quad \quad \quad \quad\downarrow \\
\quad \quad \text { 重参数化采样 }\\
\quad \quad \quad \quad\downarrow \\
x_{t}=\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t}} * x_{0}+\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}} * \varepsilon
\end{array}
$$

- 反向生成过程

  从一个高斯分布采样，通过反转过程生成观测图像

  $P_{\theta}\left(x_{0}\right)=\int p_{\theta}\left(x_{0: T}\right) d x_{1: T} \\$  表示需要模型去预测的东西（我们的这个最终的清晰图像，是以$p\left(x_{T}\right)$高斯噪声为起点
  $p_{\theta}\left(x_{0: T}\right)=p\left(x_{T}\right) * p_{\theta}\left(x_{T-1} \mid x_{T}\right) * p_{\theta}\left(x_{T-2} \mid x_{T-1}\right) * \cdots * p_{\theta}\left(x_{1} \mid x_{0}\right)$

   $p_{\theta}\left(x_{t-1} \mid x_{t}\right) $ 定义为高斯分布

  $p_{\theta}\left(x_{t-1} \mid x_{t}\right)=\mathrm{N}\left(x_{t-1} ; \mu_{\theta}\left(x_{t}, t\right), \sum_{\theta}\left(x_{t}, t\right)\right)$

![image-20251023144123927](C:\Users\admin\my-notes\notes\assets\image-20251023144123927.png)

事实上，在已知 $x_{0}$ 情况下，反向生成过程是一个确定性的过程

根据贝叶斯公式  $q\left(x_{t-1} \mid x_{t}\right)=\frac{q\left(x_{t} \mid x_{t-1}\right) q\left(x_{t-1}\right)}{q\left(x_{t}\right)} $ (为什么用q，跟网络没有关系)

$\frac{q\left(x_{t} \mid x_{t-1}, x_{0}\right) q\left(x_{t-1} \mid x_{0}\right)}{q\left(x_{t} \mid x_{0}\right)} \propto e^{-\frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{\left(x_{t}-\sqrt{a_{t}} x_{t-1}\right)^{2}}{\beta_{t}}+\frac{\left(x_{t-1}-\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}} x_{0}\right)^{2}}{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}-\frac{\left(x_{t}-\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t}} x_{0}\right)^{2}}{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}\right)}\right]}$

 $q\left(x_{t-1}\right), q\left(x_{t}\right) $ 未知，但是  $q\left(x_{t} \mid x_{0}\right) q\left(x_{t-1} \mid x_{0}\right)  $已知

$q\left(x_{t-1} \mid x_{t}, x_{0}\right)=\frac{q\left(x_{t} \mid x_{t-1}, x_{0}\right) q\left(x_{t-1} \mid x_{0}\right)}{q\left(x_{t} \mid x_{0}\right)}\\\propto e^{-\frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{\left(x_{t}-\sqrt{\alpha_{t}} x_{t-1}\right)^{2}}{\beta_{t}}+\frac{\left(x_{t-1}-\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}} x_{0}\right)^{2}}{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}-\frac{\left(x_{t}-\sqrt{\overline{\alpha_{t}}} x_{0}\right)^{2}}{\left(1-\overline{\alpha_{t}}\right)}\right]}$ 

$\begin{array}{l}
= e^{-\frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{x_{t}^{2}-2 \sqrt{\alpha_{t}} x_{t-1} x_{t}+\alpha_{t} x_{t-1}^{2}}{\beta_{t}}+\frac{x_{t-1}^{2}-2 \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}} x_{0} x_{t-1}+\bar{\alpha}_{t-1} x_{0}^{2}}{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}-\frac{\left(x_{t}-\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t}} x_{0}\right)^{2}}{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}\right)}\right]} \\
=e^{-\frac{1}{2}\left[\left(\frac{\alpha_{t}}{\beta_{t}}+\frac{1}{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}\right) x_{t-1}^{2}-2 \cdot\left(\frac{\sqrt{\alpha_{t}} x_{t}}{\beta_{t}}+\frac{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}} x_{0}}{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}\right) x_{t-1}+C\left(x_{t}, x_{0}\right)\right]} \\
\sim N\left(x_{t-1} ; \tilde{\mu}\left(x_{t}, x_{0}\right), \tilde{\sigma}_{t} l\right)
\end{array}$

$\begin{array}{l}
q\left(x_{t} \mid x_{t-1}\right)=N\left(x_{t} ; \sqrt{1-\beta_{t}} * x_{t-1}, \beta_{t} I\right) \\
q\left(x_{t} \mid x_{0}\right)=N\left(x_{t} ; \sqrt{\overline{\alpha_{t}}} * x_{0},\left(1-\overline{\alpha_{t}}\right) I\right) \\
q\left(x_{t-1} \mid x_{0}\right)=N\left(x_{t-1} ; \sqrt{\overline{\alpha_{t-1}}} * x_{0},\left(1-\overline{\alpha_{t-1}} I\right)\right.
\end{array}$

$\begin{array}{l}
\frac{q\left(x_{t} \mid x_{t-1}, x_{0}\right) q\left(x_{t-1} \mid x_{0}\right)}{q\left(x_{t} \mid x_{0}\right)} \propto e^{-\frac{1}{2}\left[\left(\frac{\alpha_{t}}{\beta_{t}}+\frac{1}{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}\right) x_{t-1}^{2}-2 *\left(\frac{\sqrt{\alpha_{t}} x_{t}}{\beta_{t}}+\frac{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}} x_{0}}{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}\right) x_{t-1}+C\left(x_{t}, x_{0}\right)\right]} \\
\tilde{\sigma}_{t}=\frac{1}{\left(\frac{\alpha_{t}}{\beta_{t}}+\frac{1}{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}\right)}=\frac{1}{\left(\frac{\alpha_{t}-\bar{\alpha}_{t}+\beta_{t}}{\beta_{t}\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}=\beta_{t} * \frac{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}}\right.} \\
\begin{aligned}
\tilde{\mu}\left(x_{t}, x_{0}\right) & =\frac{\left(\frac{\sqrt{\alpha_{t}} x_{t}}{\beta_{t}}+\frac{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}} x_{0}}{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}\right)}{\left(\frac{\alpha_{t}}{\beta_{t}}+\frac{1}{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}\right)}=\left(\frac{\sqrt{\alpha_{t}} x_{t}}{\beta_{t}}+\frac{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}} x_{0}}{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}\right) * \beta_{t} * \frac{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}} \\
& =\frac{\sqrt{\alpha_{t}} *\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}} x_{t}+\frac{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}} \beta_{t}}{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}} x_{0} \\
& =\frac{\sqrt{\alpha_{t}} *\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}} x_{t}+\frac{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}} \beta_{t}}{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t}}}\left(x_{t}-\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}} * \varepsilon_{t}\right) \\
& =\frac{\sqrt{\alpha_{t}} *\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)}{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}} x_{t}+\frac{\beta_{t}}{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_{t}}}\left(x_{t}-\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}} * \varepsilon_{t}\right) \\
& =\frac{\alpha_{t} *\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}\right)+\beta_{t}}{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}}_{t}\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}\right)} x_{t}-\frac{\beta_{t} * \sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}}}{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}\right) * \sqrt{\alpha_{t}}} \varepsilon_{t} \\
& =\frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_{t}}}\left(x_{t}-\frac{\left(1-\alpha_{t}\right)}{\sqrt{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}\right)}} \varepsilon_{t}\right)
\end{aligned}
\end{array}$

$\begin{array}{l}
\text { 已知 } x_{t}=\sqrt{\overline{\alpha_{t}}} * x_{0}+\sqrt{1-\overline{\alpha_{t}}} * \varepsilon_{t} \\
\text { 即 } x_{0}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_{t}}}\left(x_{t}-\sqrt{1-\overline{\alpha_{t}}} * \varepsilon_{t}\right)
\end{array}$

前向扩散： $q\left(x_{t} \mid x_{t-1}\right)=N\left(x_{t} ; \sqrt{1-\beta_{t}} * x_{t-1}, \beta_{t} I\right)$

反向生成： $q\left(x_{t-1} \mid x_{t}, x_{0}\right)=N\left(x_{t} ; \frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_{t}}}\left(x_{t}-\frac{\left(1-\alpha_{t}\right)}{\sqrt{\left(1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}\right)}} \varepsilon_{t}\right), \beta_{t} * \frac{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t-1}}{1-\bar{\alpha}_{t}} I\right) $

#### 优化目标

极大似然估计

从一个高斯分布采样，通过反转过程生成观测图像
$P_{\theta}\left(x_{0}\right)=\int p_{\theta}\left(x_{0: T}\right) d x_{1: T} \\$
$p_{\theta}\left(x_{0: T}\right)=p\left(x_{t}\right) * p_{\theta}\left(x_{t-1} \mid x_{t}\right) * p_{\theta}\left(x_{t-2} \mid x_{t-1}\right) * \cdots * p_{\theta}\left(x_{1} \mid x_{0}\right)$

 $p_{\theta}\left(x_{t-1} \mid x_{t}\right)$  定义为高斯分布

$p_{\theta}\left(x_{t-1} \mid x_{t}\right)=\mathrm{N}\left(x_{t-1} ; \mu_{\theta}\left(x_{t}, t\right), \sum_{\theta}\left(x_{t}, t\right)\right)$



目标：最小化负对数似然

$\mathrm{L}=-\log \left(P_{\theta}\left(x_{\theta}\right)\right) $
$$
\begin{aligned}
\log \left(P_{\theta}(x)\right) & =\log \left(P_{\theta}(x)\right) * \int q(z \mid x) d z \\
& =\int \log \left(\frac{P_{\theta}(x, z)}{P_{\theta}(z \mid x)}\right) \mathrm{q}(\mathrm{z} \mid \mathrm{x}) \mathrm{dz} \\
& =\int \log \left(\frac{P_{\theta}(x, z)}{q(z \mid x)} * \frac{q(z \mid x)}{P_{\theta}(z \mid x)}\right) \mathrm{q}(\mathrm{z} \mid \mathrm{x}) \mathrm{dz} \\
& =\int \log \left(\frac{P_{\theta}(x, z)}{q(z \mid x)}\right) \mathrm{q}(\mathrm{z} \mid \mathrm{x}) \mathrm{dz}+\operatorname{KL}\left(\mathrm{q}(\mathrm{z} \mid \mathrm{x}) \| \mathrm{P}_{\theta}(\mathrm{z} \mid \mathrm{x})\right) \\
& \geq \int \log \left(\frac{P_{\theta}(x, z)}{q(z \mid x)}\right) \mathrm{q}(\mathrm{z} \mid \mathrm{x}) \mathrm{dz} \\
& =\mathrm{E}_{\mathrm{q}(\mathrm{z} \mid \mathrm{x})}\left[\log \left(\frac{P_{\theta}(x, z)}{q(z \mid x)}\right)\right]
\end{aligned}
$$

对于DDPM

$\log \left(P_{\theta}(x)\right) \geq \mathrm{E}_{q\left(x_{1: T} \mid x_{0}\right)}\left[\log \left(\frac{p_{\theta}\left(x_{0: T}\right)}{q\left(x_{1: T} \mid x_{0}\right)}\right)\right]$


所以负对数似然L为

$L_{v l b}=\mathrm{E}_{q\left(x_{1: T} \mid x_{0}\right)}\left[\log \left(\frac{q\left(x_{1: T} \mid x_{0}\right)}{p_{\theta}\left(x_{0: T}\right)}\right)\right]$


$$
\begin{aligned}
L_{\mathrm{VLB}} & =\mathbb{E}_{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{0 T}\right)}\left[\log \frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{1: T} \mid \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}{p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{0: T}\right)}\right] \\
& =\mathbb{E}_{q}\left[\log \frac{\prod_{t=1}^{T} q\left(\mathbf{x}_{t} \mid \mathbf{x}_{t-1}\right)}{p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{T}\right) \prod_{t=1}^{T} p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{t-1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{t}\right)}\right] \\
& =\mathbb{E}_{q}\left[-\log p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{T}\right)+\sum_{t=1}^{T} \log \frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{t} \mid \mathbf{x}_{t-1}\right)}{p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{t-1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{t}\right)}\right] \\
& =\mathbb{E}_{q}\left[-\log p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{T}\right)+\sum_{t=2}^{T} \log \frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{t} \mid \mathbf{x}_{t-1}\right)}{p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{t-1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{t}\right)}+\log \frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}{p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{0} \mid \mathbf{x}_{1}\right)}\right] \\
& =\mathbb{E}_{q}\left[-\log p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{T}\right)+\sum_{t=2}^{T} \log \left(\frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{t-1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{t}, \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}{p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{t-1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{t}\right)} \cdot \frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{t} \mid \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{t-1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}\right)+\log \frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}{p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{0} \mid \mathbf{x}_{1}\right)}\right] \\
& =\mathbb{E}_{q}\left[-\log p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{T}\right)+\sum_{t=2}^{T} \log \frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{t-1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{t}, \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}{p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{t-1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{t}\right)}+\sum_{t=2}^{T} \log \frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{t} \mid \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{t-1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}+\log \frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}{p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{0} \mid \mathbf{x}_{1}\right)}\right] \\
& =\mathbb{E}_{q}\left[-\log p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{T}\right)+\sum_{t=2}^{T} \log \frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{t-1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{t}, \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}{p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{t-1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{t}\right)}+\log \frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{T} \mid \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}+\log \frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}{p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{0} \mid \mathbf{x}_{1}\right)}\right] \\
& =\mathbb{E}_{q}\left[\log \frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{T} \mid \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}{p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{T}\right)}+\sum_{t=2}^{T} \log \frac{q\left(\mathbf{x}_{t-1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{t}, \mathbf{x}_{0}\right)}{p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{t-1} \mid \mathbf{x}_{t}\right)}-\log p_{\theta}\left(\mathbf{x}_{0} \mid \mathbf{x}_{1}\right)\right] \\
